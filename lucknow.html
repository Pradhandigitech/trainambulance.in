What Is a Train Ambulance Service in Lucknow?

 

A train ambulance is a medically supported patient transfer by rail. The patient travels by train with planned medical assistance and equipment according to their clinical requirements. Unlike regular passenger travel, the journey can be organised around the patient's medical and transportation needs.

 

The complete journey can involve:
Hospital or Home → Road Ambulance → Railway Station → Train Journey → Destination Station → Road Ambulance → Receiving Hospital or Home

 

This approach makes long distance transportation easier for families because they do not have to arrange every part of the journey separately. At Save Life Wellness, we first understand the patient's condition and then discuss the support required during transportation. Depending on the patient's requirements, arrangements may include a medical attendant, oxygen support, monitoring equipment, stretcher assistance, and road ambulance coordination. More importantly, we plan the transportation around the patient rather than simply around the destination.
