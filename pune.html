How Our Train Ambulance Service Works

 
  • Call our 24/7 helpline — Tell us the patient's condition, current hospital, and destination.
  • Medical assessment — We review whether Semi-ICU, Full ICU, or doctor-escorted transfer is right for the case.
  • Ticket & equipment arrangement — We block train berths, prepare the ICU-grade equipment, and assign the medical team. This takes at least 24 hours to arrange properly.
  • Bed-to-bed pickup — Our road ambulance picks the patient up from home or hospital and transfers them to the nearest railway station.
  • Onboard ICU care — Our paramedic (and doctor, if booked) monitors vitals continuously throughout the journey.
  • Station-to-hospital handover — On arrival, our road ambulance takes the patient directly to the destination hospital, with a full handover to the receiving medical team.
 

Why Choose Train Ambulance Over Air or Road Ambulance?

 
  • Cost-effective for long distances: A Pune-to-Delhi air ambulance can cost several lakhs. Our full ICU train ambulance covers the same distance for a fraction of the price, with equivalent life-support equipment on board.
  • More comfortable for critical patients: Long road journeys are physically taxing for unstable patients. Trains offer a smoother, more stable ride over 20+ hour journeys.
  • Full ICU capability: Whether your patient needs oxygen support alone or is on a ventilator/BiPAP, our onboard setup replicates hospital-grade ICU monitoring for the entire journey.
